## Full description (4000 char max)

```
GetDraft is where athletes get found.

Every year talented players go unseen because they don't know the right
coach and the right coach doesn't know they exist. GetDraft closes that
gap. Athletes build a profile that shows what they can actually do, and
coaches, recruiters and agents find them by sport, level and location.

HOW IT WORKS

Draft or Pass. Swipe right to Draft someone you want to hear from, left
to Pass. When you both Draft each other, it's a match — and the chat
opens. No cold emails that go nowhere, no messages from people you have
no interest in. Both sides said yes before anyone can talk.

FOR ATHLETES

• Build a profile with photos, video, position, level and class year
• Get discovered by verified coaches, recruiters and agents
• See exactly who drafted you on your Draft Board
• Track your Draft Score and your ranking in your sport
• Message recruiters directly once you match

FOR COACHES, RECRUITERS AND AGENTS

• Search real athletes by sport, position, level and country
• See verified profiles with film, not just stats on paper
• Build a roster of the athletes you're tracking
• Reach out the moment a Draft is mutual

RANKINGS

Every athlete gets a Draft Score based on real activity — profile
strength, interest received and verification. See where you stand in
your sport in your country, and on the world board across every country.

THE GLOBE

Talent is not evenly distributed and neither is opportunity. The Globe
shows athletes and recruiters on a live world map, so a coach in Boston
can find a prospect in Algiers and an athlete anywhere can see who is
looking.

BUILT FOR YOUNGER ATHLETES TOO

Under-18 athletes can link a parent or guardian, who sees the recruiter
outreach and stays in the loop. Identity verification keeps the platform
credible for everyone on it.

FREE TO START

Create a profile, get discovered and see who drafted you for free.
Upgrade for more monthly Drafts, Super Drafts and priority placement.

Sports covered include soccer, basketball, football, baseball, hockey,
volleyball, tennis, golf, swimming, track and field, MMA and more,
across high school, CEGEP, NCAA, U SPORTS and pro pathways.

Get scouted. Get drafted.
```

*~2,050 characters — well inside the 4,000 limit.*
