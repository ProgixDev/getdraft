# Setting up support@getdraft.net

**For:** Patrick Charles
**Time needed:** about 2 minutes
**Cost:** nothing — see below

---

## The short version

You do **not** need to buy anything.

`support@getdraft.net` can be added as an **alias** on your existing Google
Workspace account. An alias is a second address that delivers into the mailbox
you already use. Google does not charge for aliases — you can have up to 30 on
a single account.

You would only pay if you created a **new user**, which is not necessary here.

---

## Why it is needed

The address is published in four places:

| Where | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Google Play store listing | The support contact users see |
| Privacy Policy | Legally required contact point |
| Terms of Service | Same |
| **Account deletion page** | **Required by Google Play** |

That last one is the important one. Google requires any app where people can
create an account to publish a page explaining how to delete it. Ours tells
users to email `support@getdraft.net` if they have already uninstalled the app.

Right now that address does not exist, so those emails bounce back with an
error. Google checks this page during review.

---

## How to add it

1. Go to **admin.google.com** and sign in with your GetDraft Workspace account

2. In the left menu: **Directory** → **Users**

3. Click **your own user account** in the list

4. Find **User information** → **Alternate email addresses (email aliases)**
   (on some versions it is just called *Email aliases*)

5. Click **Add an alternate email**

6. Type `support` in the box — the `@getdraft.net` part is added automatically

7. Click **Save**

That is all. Anything sent to `support@getdraft.net` will now arrive in your
normal inbox, alongside your usual mail.

**It can take up to 24 hours to activate**, though it is usually working within
a few minutes.

---

## How to check it worked

Send an email to `support@getdraft.net` from any other address — your phone,
a personal Gmail, anything.

- **It arrives in your inbox** → done, nothing else needed
- **You get a bounce** saying *"Address not found"* → the alias has not
  activated yet. Wait an hour and try once more.

Please tell us either way, so we know the deletion page is working before
Google finishes reviewing the app.

---

## If you would rather not use an alias

Two alternatives, both fine:

**A group instead of an alias** — Admin console → **Directory** → **Groups** →
*Create group* → name it `support`. Useful later if more than one person should
receive support email.

**A different address** — if you already have something like
`contact@getdraft.net` or `info@getdraft.net` working, tell us which one and we
will change it everywhere instead. The website pages update immediately, and the
address shown inside the app updates over the air, so users get it without
needing to install anything. It does not delay the Play Store review.

## Questions you might have

**Will this create a new mailbox I have to check separately?**
No. An alias delivers into your existing inbox. There is nothing new to sign
into.

**Can I reply from it?**
Yes. In Gmail: Settings → *Accounts* → *Send mail as* → add
`support@getdraft.net`. Then you can choose it as the sender when replying.

**Does this cost extra per month?**
No. Aliases are included in every Google Workspace plan.

**What happens if I do nothing?**
The app still works, but anyone emailing support gets a bounce, and the
account-deletion route Google requires is only half working. It is worth the
two minutes.
