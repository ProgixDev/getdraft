Setting up support@getdraft.net

GetDraft · about 2 minutes · no cost

You do not need to buy anything.
support@getdraft.net can be added as an alias on the Google Workspace account you already have. An alias is a second address that delivers into your existing inbox. Google includes up to 30 aliases per account at no charge. You would only pay if you created a new user, which is not needed here.

Why it is needed

This address is published in four places:

WhereWhy it matters
Google Play store listingThe support contact users see
Privacy PolicyLegally required contact point
Terms of ServiceSame
Account deletion page Required by Google Play

The last one matters most. Google requires any app where people create an account to publish a page explaining how to delete it. Ours tells users to email support@getdraft.net if they have already uninstalled the app.

Right now that address does not exist. Emails sent to it bounce back with “Address not found”. Google checks this page while reviewing the app.

How to add it

  1. Go to admin.google.com and sign in with your GetDraft Workspace account
  2. In the left menu: Directory → Users
  3. Click your own user account in the list
  4. Open User information → Alternate email addresses (email aliases)
    On some versions this is simply called Email aliases
  5. Click Add an alternate email
  6. Type support — the @getdraft.net part is filled in automatically
  7. Click Save

That is all. Anything sent to support@getdraft.net now arrives in your normal inbox, alongside your usual mail. Activation is usually a few minutes, though Google allows up to 24 hours.

How to check it worked

Send an email to support@getdraft.net from any other address — your phone, a personal Gmail, anything.

  • It arrives in your inbox → done, nothing else needed
  • You get a bounce saying “Address not found” → not active yet. Wait an hour and try once more.

Please tell us either way, so we know the deletion page works before Google finishes its review.

If you would rather not use an alias

A group instead

Admin console → Directory → Groups → Create group, named support. Worth considering if more than one person should receive support email later.

A different address

If you already have something like contact@getdraft.net or info@getdraft.net working, tell us which and we will change it everywhere instead. That is a small change on our side and does not require a new version of the app.

Questions you might have

Will this create a new mailbox I have to check separately?
No. An alias delivers into your existing inbox. Nothing new to sign into.
Can I reply from it?
Yes. In Gmail: Settings → Accounts → Send mail as → add support@getdraft.net. You can then choose it as the sender.
Does it cost extra per month?
No. Aliases are included in every Google Workspace plan.
What happens if I do nothing?
The app still works, but anyone emailing support gets a bounce, and the account-deletion route Google requires is only half working.
