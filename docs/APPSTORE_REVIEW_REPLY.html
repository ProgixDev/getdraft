Replying to Apple App Review

GetDraft · Guideline 2.1 — Information Needed

This is not a rejection. Guideline 2.1 “Information Needed” is a standard request on a first submission. Apple has paused the review and is waiting for answers. Once you reply with everything below, the review continues.

Apple asked seven questions. The answers to questions 2–7 are written out below — copy each grey box into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes. Question 1 is a video you need to record.

3. What the app does and who it is for

GetDraft is a sports recruiting platform that connects athletes with coaches, recruiters and agents. THE PROBLEM Talented athletes go unrecruited because they have no direct line to the coaches looking for them, and recruiters cannot efficiently find prospects outside their existing networks. Recruiting today depends on personal connections, expensive showcase events, and geography. HOW IT WORKS Athletes build a profile with their sport, position, level, class year, photos and highlight video. Coaches, recruiters and agents browse athlete profiles filtered by sport, position, level and location. Either side can express interest by "Drafting" the other (swipe right) or passing (swipe left). When both sides Draft each other it becomes a match, and only then does messaging open. Nobody can be contacted without having first expressed interest in the person contacting them. The app also includes a world ranking board for each sport, based on profile strength and interest received, and a map showing where athletes and recruiters are located. WHO IT IS FOR - Athletes aged 13 and over, from high school to professional level - Coaches, recruiters and agents at schools, colleges, clubs and agencies Athletes under 18 must link a parent or guardian, who is notified of recruiter interest and whose approval activates the account.

6. Regional differences

The app works exactly the same in every region. There are no region-specific features, content or restrictions. Content is not filtered by country: users can browse athletes and recruiters worldwide, and the ranking board covers every country. The app is available in English only.

7. Regulated industry or protected material

GetDraft does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no protected third-party material. There are no team logos, league marks, broadcast footage or licensed sports data in the app. All photos and video are uploaded by the users themselves. Identity verification is provided by Didit, an identity verification company we hold a commercial account with. We do not handle or store identity documents ourselves. Didit processes them and returns only the result of the check. Regarding younger users: the app is rated 13+, and athletes under 18 must link a parent or guardian, who is notified of recruiter interest and whose approval is required before the account becomes active. Messaging only becomes possible after both people have expressed interest in each other, so no adult can start a conversation with a minor uninvited.

Before you reply, check these

  • Test on a real iPhone. Apple states in its message that apps are reviewed on physical devices. This is the most common cause of a second round of questions.
  • App Store screenshots must show the app in use — not the login screen, splash screen or title art. Apple mentions this explicitly.
  • Check the permission messages. Each one must clearly say why the app needs access, and give an example of how the information is used.
  • Paste answers 2–7 into the Notes field, not only into the reply message, so they are saved for future submissions too.
